FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What language are courses taught in? +
Materials are in English and German. Q&A sessions can be held in English, German, Turkish, Azerbaijani, or Russian. The Mastery course includes dedicated German-language reporting practice.
Are these CME-accredited courses? +
Currently not formally CME-accredited. These are continuing education resources developed by a German-trained physician to practical diagnostic standards. Formal accreditation is planned for future iterations.
How long does access last after purchasing? +
All courses include lifetime access to all video and PDF materials, including any future updates. Community access varies by tier: 6 months for Clinical, lifetime for Mastery.
Can I upgrade from a lower tier later? +
Yes. If you purchase Pilot and later want Clinical or Mastery, you only pay the difference. Contact us at hello@radioloq.com to arrange the upgrade — we'll apply your credit immediately.
What does the letter of recommendation involve? +
Upon completing Mastery with demonstrated proficiency — assessed through the mock exam and case submissions — Dr. Eddy can provide a personalised letter for fellowship or hospital position applications. This is awarded on merit, not automatically upon enrollment.
Is there a refund policy? +
Yes. If you're unsatisfied within 14 days of purchase and have completed fewer than 2 modules, we offer a full refund — no questions asked. Contact hello@radioloq.com.
I'm already a radiology resident — is this still useful? +
Yes, especially the Clinical and Mastery tiers. The structured reporting templates, German reporting practice, and mock Facharzt exam are designed specifically for residents who want to formalize and accelerate their development.
